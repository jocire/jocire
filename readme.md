I work at the intersection of customers and engineering: reproducing difficult issues, isolating root causes, translating customer reports into actionable technical requirements, and building tools that make troubleshooting and support workflows more effective.

## Featured project

A WordPress troubleshooting and diagnostics project designed to investigate production-site issues while minimizing impact on normal users. It explores session-isolated troubleshooting, evidence collection, diagnostics, compatibility analysis, and support-engineering workflows.

My broader interest is building practical tooling that helps support and engineering teams move from vague problem reports to reliable evidence and actionable technical conclusions.
